## This Guide Is for Complete Beginners

If you are completely new to mod development, then this guide is perfect for you! The guide will teach you from the very basics, so no prior knowledge is needed! 

The linguistic style of this guide is friendly and natural, aiming to make you feel like a conversation while learning. We will not throw a bunch of terminologies at you without explanation.

Instead, we will explain necessary ones, and teach you through hand-on projects and examples.

No need to be afraid!

## Guide & Parties

## Why Learning Modding?

What can you do with mod development? Well, it is a fun and cool way to implement your ideas into a world with infinite possibilities. Introduce your own culture? Your imagined heroes or weapons? Stories of Legends? Or perhaps you hope to make a positive impact in our community with the power of mods? We offer the door to all that.
